## Background & Expertise
Raj Patil is an aerospace systems architect and artificial intelligence researcher based in Bengaluru, India. He pioneered the OrbitNet-Bio neural transformer model capable of achieving 0.42 ms single-cell classification latency on satellite-embedded FPGA accelerators. Under his leadership, Drexorium Labs conducts advanced research in GSLV-MK3 heavy launch vehicles, cryogenic liquid engines, extremophile microgravity biology (*Deinococcus radiodurans*), and 100 FPS edge-AI scouting drones.
