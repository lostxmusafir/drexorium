# Technology & Research Overview — Drexorium Labs

## Integrated Aerospace AI & Software Concepts
Drexorium Labs develops AI software and research concepts for aerospace applications, exploring heavy orbital launch concepts, deep space telemetry, microgravity biological payload research, and autonomous scouting drones.

## Core Software & Research Focus Areas
1. **GSLV-MK3 Launch Concepts**: Research models for 4,000 kg GTO heavy payload launcher architectures and cryogenic stages.
2. **OrbitNet-Bio AI**: 0.42 ms latency transformer neural model research concept co-developed with **Zydrakon AI**.
3. **AeroMars Scout Drone**: 100 FPS edge-AI computer vision for GPS-denied planetary mapping.
4. **Microfluidic Bio-Cassette**: Conceptual design for microgravity biological research modules.
