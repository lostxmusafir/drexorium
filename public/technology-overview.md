# Technology & Engineering Overview — Drexorium Labs

## Integrated Defense & Aerospace Technologies
Drexorium Labs integrates hardware engineering with neural AI software architectures to pioneer heavy orbital launches, deep space telemetry, microgravity biological payload incubation, and autonomous scouting drones.

## Key Hardware & Software Assets
1. **GSLV-MK3 Launch Vehicle**: 4,000 kg GTO heavy payload launcher with C25 LH2/LOX cryogenic stage.
2. **OrbitNet-Bio AI**: 0.42 ms latency transformer neural model co-developed with **Zydrakon AI**.
3. **AeroMars Scout Drone**: 100 FPS edge-AI computer vision for GPS-denied planetary mapping.
4. **Microfluidic Bio-Cassette**: Sealed ISO-14644 Class 5 cleanroom incubator for microgravity biological research.
